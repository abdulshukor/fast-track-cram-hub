# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, collaborative, and specific. Welcome contributors with different experience levels. Discuss technical decisions and content accuracy without personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, or intimidation. Do not publish another person's credentials, private study history or personal information.

Maintainers may edit, lock or remove harmful contributions and restrict participation to protect the project. Report issues privately to maintainers through the repository's contact information; do not post sensitive allegations or personal data in a public issue.
